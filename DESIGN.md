# Design direction

## World

An engineer's field notebook: dark carbon surfaces, warm paper-like text, rust accents, and small operational labels. Technical cues support the story; they do not replace it.

## Dials

- DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7
- MOTION_INTENSITY: 5
- VISUAL_DENSITY: 4

## Tokens

- Ink: #141514
- Panel: #1d211f
- Paper: #f1ede5
- Muted: #a8aaa3
- Rust: #d56a42
- Line: rgba(241, 237, 229, 0.14)
- Display: "DM Sans", sans-serif
- Technical: "IBM Plex Mono", monospace

## Composition

The first viewport leads with a direct professional statement, a short proof line, and one primary contact action. Projects use an editorial index with a large lead case and compact supporting cases. Experience reads as a vertical record rather than a card grid.

## Motion

One reveal sequence on load, restrained hover movement on project rows, and a subtle cursor-following signal on desktop only. All motion is disabled or reduced under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Anti-references

Avoid full-page terminal cosplay, purple gradients, nested cards, generic three-column feature grids, emojis as icons, and text-heavy hero sections.
